title: Features
description: Every feature of Protobase, grouped by area, with whether it is stable.
---

Every feature of Protobase is listed on the page of its area, one line per feature: its name and what it gives you. A linked name leads to the page that explains it in full.

A feature without a mark is stable. The others say so after their name:

- **beta**: built, released and usable, with the limits its page lists. It may change more than a stable feature.
- **in development**: partly released, not usable on its own yet.
- **coming soon**: documented, not built yet.

## Areas

- [Start a project](/features/start-a-project/): Start a new app, or an admin over an existing Postgres database, and keep the config in step with it.
- [Data model](/features/data-model/): Describe the tables of an existing Postgres database as resources, with their fields, keys and rules.
- [Access](/features/access/): Decide who may read and change which records and fields.
- [Lists](/features/lists/): The list page of each resource, with its columns, filters, charts, search, exports and imports.
- [List displays](/features/list-displays/): Show a list as cards, a map, a board, a calendar or a timeline, and reorder records by hand.
- [Record pages](/features/record-pages/): The page of one record, with its sections, related records, galleries, charts and editing.
- [Actions](/features/actions/): Buttons that change records, ask for input or run your own code, per role.
- [Composed pages](/features/composed-pages/): Pages of your own, built from blocks in a JSX layout.
- [Your own code](/features/your-own-code/): React components, field types, pages and handlers of your own, loaded as plugins.
- [Look and language](/features/look-and-language/): The app's colours, name, icon, language and installation.
- [Navigation](/features/navigation/): The sidebar, the user menu, the bottom bar on phones and the global search.
- [REST API](/features/rest-api/): The API every resource gets, with filters, writes, errors and the typed client.
- [API functions](/features/api-functions/): Endpoints of your own beside the REST API.
- [Sign-in and accounts](/features/sign-in-and-accounts/): Sign-in methods, users, roles, organizations and tokens.
- [Files and images](/features/files-and-images/): Uploaded files and images, where they are kept and how they are served.
- [History, comments and live updates](/features/history-comments-and-live-updates/): The changes of each record, comments on it, and pages that refresh as records change.
- [Events, webhooks and jobs](/features/events-webhooks-and-jobs/): React to changes, deliver them to other systems, and run work in the background.
- [Notifications](/features/notifications/): A bell in the top bar and push to browsers and phones, for what your app tells its users.
- [Calls](/features/calls/): Audio and video calls between an app's users, with Protobase deciding who is in a call and who hears whom.
- [Email and public forms](/features/email-and-public-forms/): Mail the app sends, the PDF documents it makes, and forms for people without an account.
- [AI](/features/ai/): Fields and actions filled by a model, the assistant beside the admin, and MCP.
- [Compatibility and moving from other systems](/features/compatibility/): Apps written for other systems that keep working on Protobase, and moving their data and code over.
- [Several apps](/features/several-apps/): Show another Protobase app's records and accept its users.
- [Build and run](/features/build-and-run/): Develop, build and run an app, on servers, in containers and on phones.
- [Releases](/features/releases/): How Protobase is released and versioned, and what a release keeps working.
