---
title: Record pages
description: The page of one record, with its sections, related records, galleries, charts and editing.
---

- [Record page layout](/reference/ui-config/#the-record-page): Sections, a summary sidebar, related tables, maps, charts, galleries, history, comments, conversations and your own components.
- [Full-width record pages](/reference/ui-config/#page-width): A view's record pages and create form fill the whole area beside the sidebar instead of a centred column, with the sidebar cards still beside the sections.
- [Focus pages](/reference/ui-config/#focus-pages) (beta): A record whose page is one big surface, such as a graph or a board, fills the page under a thin bar with its Save, actions and panels, its form a link away.
- [A create address that opens a page](/reference/ui-config/#focus-pages) (beta): `.createOpens(to)` sends a resource's create address to a page in place of its form, such as a record's focus page with a panel open, filled from the address's values.
- [Record pages that open another](/reference/ui-config/#a-record-page-that-opens-another): A record's page opens another address built from its fields in its place, such as a workflow run opening its workflow's page with the run shown.
- [Full-area record pages](/reference/ui-config/#full-area-record-pages) (beta): A view draws its record pages with a component of its own that fills the area beside the sidebar and under the top bar, such as a chat channel, and links to the standard page.
- [Read view and edit](/reference/ui-config/#read-view-and-edit): A record opens as a read-only fact sheet or a form, and Edit opens the form at its own address.
- [Unsaved edits](/reference/ui-config/#editing-a-record): Unsaved changes to a record stay in the browser and can be resumed, and leaving a form, a dialog or a comment with changes asks first.
- [Record forms](/reference/ui-config/#editing-a-record): Relations are picked from a list that narrows as you type, numbers are read as the browser's language writes them, and a refused save says under each field what is wrong.
- [Titles per reader](/reference/ui-config/#titles-per-reader): A record without a name of its own, such as a direct message, is titled for each reader from its fields, such as after the other members, in the sidebar, heading, browser tab, relation links, list columns, relation pickers, filters and search.
- [JSON fields on forms](/reference/plugins/#json) (beta): A JSON field, such as a workflow run's input, is edited on forms as JSON text and shown written out on several lines.
- [Member fields](/reference/plugins/#members): A field that holds a list of people, such as a private channel's members, picks the organization's members by name on forms and shows them by avatar and name.
- [Save feedback](/reference/ui-config/#the-record-page): A view shows saving on the Save button instead of in a toast.
- [Save conflicts](/reference/ui-config/#editing-a-record): When someone else changed a record while you edited it, Save shows their value beside yours for each field you both changed, and you keep yours or take theirs.
- [Groups and tabs](/reference/ui-config/#groups): Parts of a record page sit under headings that open on click, or in tabs, and the open ones are kept in the address.
- [Key facts](/reference/ui-config/#key-facts): A section shows its values side by side with icons.
- [Confirming a change](/reference/ui-config/#confirming-a-change): A field that is easy to set by mistake asks a question before a person changes it in a form or a list cell, and Cancel keeps its value.
- [Fields only once a record exists](/reference/ui-config/#names-and-columns): A view leaves a field off the create form, such as a choice among records added after it, and the record page shows it as usual.
- [Help while editing](/reference/ui-config/#help-while-editing): Help lines meant for whoever changes a record show only on its form.
- [Conditional sections](/reference/ui-config/#form-and-read-view): Sections and fields show only while editing, only while reading, or only when a condition on the record holds.
- [Galleries](/reference/ui-config/#galleries): A record's photos show as tiles that open a full-screen lightbox, with keyboard and swipe navigation.
- [Related records](/reference/ui-config/#related-records): Every record page shows how many records point at it, and can list related records, also through a join resource, as a table, another display or cards with buttons, each row or card with a button to an address its fields name.
- [Record lines](/reference/ui-config/#record-lines): The related table a record's view marks as its lines, such as an invoice's, shows all of them on its page, where you edit, add, remove and reorder them, and Save writes the record and its lines together.
- [Conversations](/reference/ui-config/#conversations): A record page holds a chat about the record, such as a task's, with its messages in markdown and a box that sends one, declared in its layout with no component of the app's own.
- [Charts of samples](/reference/ui-config/#charts-of-related-records): A record page graphs numeric samples over the last hour, day or week, such as a server's CPU use.
- [Fresh data on return](/reference/ui-config/#the-record-page): Records and lists reload when the browser tab is focused again, keeping unsaved edits.
- [Safe deletes](/reference/ui-config/#deleting-a-record): Deleting asks first, or offers Undo for a soft delete, offers a review when the record changed since it was opened, and names the records that still point at it.
